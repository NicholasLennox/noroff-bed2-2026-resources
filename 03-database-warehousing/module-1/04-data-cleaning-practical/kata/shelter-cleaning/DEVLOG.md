# Dev log

A running record of your work. Add an entry whenever you find something, build something or change your mind. A few lines is enough, and you don't need every heading every time.

Copy this block for each entry:

```markdown
### Entry title

- **Found**: what you noticed in the data or the output
- **Made**: what you built or wrote
- **Changed**: what you changed, and why
- **Learnt**: anything that surprised you or clicked
```

## Example entry

### Species casing

- **Found**: the sample has `Dog`, `DOG` and `dog` for the same species.
- **Made**: rule 1, `standardiseSpecies`, which makes every species lowercase.
- **Learnt**: the empty-values count in the summary doesn't show this kind of problem. I only saw it by reading the rows.
