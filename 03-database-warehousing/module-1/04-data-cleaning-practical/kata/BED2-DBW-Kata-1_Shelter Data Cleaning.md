# BED 2 Database Warehousing - Kata 1

This practical is about building a data cleaning pipeline yourself. You explore an intake export from three animal shelters, record its quality problems in a data quality report, write cleaning rules for them, and apply those rules as small functions in Node.js.

A starter project gives you the pipeline's structure, a console summary and a simple cleaning report, so you focus on the cleaning itself.

## The project

Everything is in [`shelter-cleaning/`](shelter-cleaning/). Copy the folder somewhere of your own before you start.

- [`INSTRUCTIONS.md`](shelter-cleaning/INSTRUCTIONS.md) has the scenario, the data, the three stages and the reflection questions. Start here.
- [`cleaning-rules.md`](shelter-cleaning/cleaning-rules.md) is where your data quality report and your rules go.
- [`DEVLOG.md`](shelter-cleaning/DEVLOG.md) is your dev log.

The [Data Cleaning and Preprocessing lecture](../../03-data-cleaning-and-preprocessing/lecture.md) walks through the same kind of pipeline on a sales export.
